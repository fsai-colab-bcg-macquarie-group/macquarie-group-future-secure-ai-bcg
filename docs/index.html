The firm sells operated “AI Co-Workers” into regulated workflows. The published bench is a consulting-and-buyer-bank overlay with one Cohere-shaped CTO seat. Independent proof of a second paying customer, of 10x cost cuts, of a software-engineering leadership line, and of where the 282 people sit, is not in the record below.

What the published seats were hired from — vs what the titles do

The leadership page is a prior-employer wall. McKinsey, Macquarie, BCG, and Deloitte fill founder, president, delivery, PE, and APAC seats. The Cohere mention sits on the CTO line. That mix sells trust to a bank committee. It does not, by itself, staff month-end close on a new agent runtime. Zero software-engineer seats on that page. Two seats labelled AI/ML.
