How an AI firm is built

A public-source case study of Future Secure AI — useful as an educational map of how an AI consulting-shaped product company can be assembled. Compiled 10 September 2026 from the company website, press, and job ads. Not an official history.

The pattern

  1. Founder already next to a large buyer (Macquarie Capital).
  2. Second founder who can talk to boards (former McKinsey partner).
  3. First customer is also first investor (AFR, Sep 2025).
  4. A new category name so you are not priced as SaaS or timesheets: AI Co-Workers / Red Zone.
  5. Leadership hired from the same firms as the buyers (banks, PE, BCG, Deloitte, McKinsey).
  6. HQ follows the capital: Sydney origin → Austin, April 2026.

Staff footprint (public only)

LinkedIn’s company page (third-party estimate, Sep 2026): about 282 people, +182% YoY, in Australia, Brazil, the United States, Canada, and Vietnam. The website lists eight hubs. Leadership bios on the site are concentrated in Macquarie, McKinsey, BCG, and Deloitte, with a smaller AI-platform core (Cohere, CISO, labs).

That mix is who the firm shows the market. It is not a census of delivery centres. This page does not scrape private employee profiles.
