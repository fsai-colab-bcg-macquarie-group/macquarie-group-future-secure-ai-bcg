AI Co-Workers – a new category of AI
Unlike AI agents designed for individual tasks, AI Co-Workers operate as digital colleagues that execute complex, high-volume workflows while collaborating with your people and systems. By automating operational work, they free your teams to focus on higher-value, creative and strategic activities.
‍
Built for the realities of large enterprises, our AI Co-Workers are LLM-agnostic, secure by design, resilient, and fully auditable. They integrate with your existing technology ecosystem to deliver measurable productivity gains, strategic advantage, and faster return on investment.
