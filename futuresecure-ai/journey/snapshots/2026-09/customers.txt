Built together
Every partnership begins by understanding your strategic priorities, operational challenges, and highest-value opportunities. Together, we identify and prioritize AI Co-Worker use cases, combining your organizational knowledge with our experience delivering enterprise AI across sectors and functions.
We then design, build, deploy, and continuously optimize AI Co-Workers in close collaboration with business leaders, use case owners, technology teams, and subject matter experts. From discovery through deployment and ongoing performance reporting, Future Secure AI remains a hands-on partner focused on delivering measurable business outcomes.
customer story #1
Transforming enterprise finance operations
Customer
A global financial services organization operating in 30+ markets, with more than US$700B in assets under management and approximately 200 portfolio companies.
Challenge
Accelerate finance transformation by reducing manual effort, improving productivity, strengthening governance, and supporting complex workflows in a highly regulated environment.
Our approach
Working closely with finance leaders and subject matter experts, we identified and prioritized a pipeline of AI Co-Workers for high-value workflows including tax assessment, month-end close, cash flow management, reconciliations, regulatory reporting, and employee verification. The solution was designed to meet the organization's security and compliance requirements.
Impact
Estimated annual benefits of US$20M to 30M, with the program expanding into HR, investor relations, and other business functions.
customer story #2
Improving healthcare operations at scale
Customer
A leading listed pathology and diagnostics provider with US$1Bn in annual revenue, operating a national network of laboratories and patient collection centers that process millions of tests each year.
Challenge
Identify high-value opportunities to improve operational efficiency across a large, distributed workforce while building executive alignment around an enterprise AI strategy.
Our approach
Through collaborative workshops with business leaders, we identified and prioritized AI Co-Workers for accounts receivable and workforce rostering, two areas with significant potential to improve productivity and business performance. The roadmap is now expanding to include laboratory operations, customer service, IT, and HR.
Impact
Established a prioritized pipeline of enterprise AI initiatives, with initial AI Co-Workers focused on high-value operational workflows and a clear path for expansion across the organization.
customer story #3
Creating value across an investment portfolio
Customer
A global private equity firm with approximately 200 portfolio companies, seeking to accelerate value creation through AI across its investments.
Challenge
Identify high-return AI opportunities that could improve operational performance and enterprise value across multiple portfolio companies.
Our approach
Working with portfolio company leadership teams, we identified and prioritized AI Co-Worker use cases tailored to each business. At one national waste management company, AI Co-Workers were designed to improve fleet utilization, reduce the cost to serve customers, increase customer contact capacity, and strengthen revenue collection.
Impact
Early results include improved compliance with bin hire periods and payment processes, driving higher revenue and faster cash collection. Over the expected investment holding period, the AI Co-Workers are projected to deliver an approximately 10% uplift in bottom-line performance.
