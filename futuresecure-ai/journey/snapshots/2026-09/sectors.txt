Private Equity
Private Equity includes portfolios of businesses and investments owned by private equity, sovereign wealth funds, and venture capital. These owners of assets focus on the opportunity to rapidly scale solutions to drive value across their whole portfolio, recognizing the heterogeneous nature of the technology architecture across their companies. AI Co-Workers can significantly enhance the value of portfolio companies over the holding period.

HR Recruitment and Onboarding AI-Co-Worker team
The Opportunity
Recruitment and onboarding issues such as position vacancy days, speed to competency, and HR team capacity constraints have a direct impact on EBITDA and operational efficiency.
Our Solution
A team of four HR Recruitment and Onboarding AI Co-Workers connect multiple data sources and approval workflows. They quickly turn resignations into approved requisitions, accelerate sourcing-to-offer, manage onboarding, and provide insights to leaders.

75% cycle reduction time with a 12% EBITDA uplift for a single geography business unit, with the ability to replicate globally.

Banking
Banking provides financial infrastructure for deposits, lending, and payments. This sector has major opportunities to modernize decisioning, compliance, and servicing through data-driven operations and connected digital ecosystems.
case study #1
Employee Expense Assistant AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
Expense reporting is required in every organization, but manual processes cause errors, delays, and productivity loss as employees navigate complex policies and rework submissions.
Our Solution
An Expense Assistant AI Co-Worker, integrated in Microsoft Teams, completes expense entry, receipt uploads, and policy checks on behalf of employees, generating compliant reports routed through the company’s expense system.
The Impact
65% time saving across expense creation, review, approval, and audit. Investment in the AI Co-Worker recovered within seven months. Improved compliance and employee satisfaction through faster reimbursement.
case study #2
Account Switching
AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
Account switch teams handle high volumes of emails each month to move customers between accounts. Most steps remain manual, with data re-entered across systems and customer messages drafted by hand. This drives long handling times, rework, and inconsistent customer experiences.
Our Solution
An Account Switching AI Co-Worker reads and classifies every switch email, sets up the case and folders, updates core systems, structures payment information for loading, and drafts customer and banker messages for a human to review and send.
The Impact
Account switches are reliably automated and completed in minutes. Improved consistency in customer communication, reducing rework and handling times. Higher switching volumes supported without an increase in headcount.
case study #3
Trade Surveillance
AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
Applying surveillance rules to every compiled alert requires manual evaluation of price movements, customer behavior, follow-on trades, and order conditions before producing auditable conclusions. At scale, this is inconsistent and slow, increasing regulatory risk and downstream rework.
Our Solution
An AI Worker systematically applies the full rule set to each alert, evaluating timestamps, price movements, customer behaviour, relationships, and order conditions. It generates a structured output per alert covering conclusions, drivers, risks, decisions, and audit traceability, written back to the case management system with supervisor-in-the-loop review maintained throughout.
The Impact
Consistent, policy-aligned rule application at scale. Faster alert clearance, a full audit trail, and a five-day SLA achieved without headcount growth.
case study #4
Financial Crime AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
Analysts complete due-diligence reviews manually, working through long checklists and gathering evidence from disconnected systems. Evidence is hard to locate and reviews vary between analysts, driving high workloads and slow turnarounds.
Our Solution
A Financial Crime AI Co-Worker classifies each case and its review scope, gathers and extracts evidence across systems, runs know-your-customer and screening checks, flags evidence gaps, and drafts customer outreach with a confidence score, leaving the exit decision with its supervisor.
The Impact
Automated data gathering leaves the team to focus on applying judgement and customer interaction. Improved review consistency and completeness reduces rework and improved quality control. Audit traceability across every case is strengthened.

Insurance
The insurance sector continues to advance through smarter data use, faster claims resolution, and more adaptive risk models, protecting people, businesses, and assets from uncertainty. It provides many operational and functional AI opportunities for both greater productivity and better decision-making.
case study #1
A Pre-Approval AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
An insurance company processes 150 pre-approval claim requests each day through a heavily manual workflow. Skilled assessors monitor inboxes and forms, re-enter data, verify policies, calculate outcomes, and draft customer communications, limiting the time spent on high-value judgment.
The Solution
A Pre-Approval AI Co-Worker runs the claim request end-to-end. It monitors inboxes and attachments, extracts and verifies customer and policy data, applies policy coverage, calculates benefits, drafts customer communications, and closes the case with an audit trail.
The Impact
60% reduction in handling time per case. Faster feedback to customers and more time for the skilled assessors to spend reviewing, approving, and making complex claims decisions.

case study #1
Fund Reconciliations
AI Co-Worker
The Challenge
Reconciling fund data requires manually extracting figures from hundreds of PDFs, Word documents, emails, and spreadsheets each month. The process consumes days of skilled analyst time and is prone to errors that create accuracy and privacy risks.
Our Solution
A Fund Reconciliations AI Co-Worker updates fund records by scanning documents in every format, using machine vision and panels of judges to ensure accuracy. It checks outgoing summaries against source data to identify errors as information moves between teams and generates an audit log highlighting data requiring human review.

An error rate reduced to zero. A process that took three days each month now takes around two hours.

Deep, insightful, and customized commentary (superior to competitors) produced for all 1,500 clients with no headcount increase.

AI Co-Worker
